      Architecture

      How it works

      Five parts, built in this order.

      A

      Collect and store

      Pulls public posts, replies, forwards, and comments into one time-stamped database, so every conversation can be replayed in order.

      Telethon, X API, PRAW, PostgreSQL with TimescaleDB
      B

      Read the emotion

      Language models label sentiment, stance, and emotions such as anxiety, excitement, and sarcasm. For replies, the original post is read too, which helps catch sarcasm.

      XLM-RoBERTa, Hugging Face Transformers
      C

      Profile the audience

      Estimates age brackets, location, language, and interests from public bios and activity. Results appear only as anonymous group totals.

      fastText, scikit-learn, zero-shot classification
      D

      Spot trends early

      Groups posts into topics, detects sudden jumps in keywords, ranks them, and forecasts the next few hours.

      BERTopic, burst detection, Prophet
      E

      Map influence

      Builds a graph of replies, mentions, reposts, and forwards to find key opinion leaders and trace how a topic moves between communities.

      NetworkX, PageRank, Louvain

      Privacy by design

      Profiling needs care, so the limits are built in.

      Public data onlyNothing from private accounts or chats.
      No individual profilesDemographics are shown as group totals.
      Small groups hiddenAny group under 20 accounts is not shown.
      Hashed identitiesUser IDs are hashed before storage.
